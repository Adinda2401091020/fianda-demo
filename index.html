A story in every roast

From roasted beans to a place to belong.

Fianda began its journey as a roasted-bean supplier in 2018, before growing into a coffee shop where people can enjoy the coffee itself, food, and the atmosphere around it.

Today, the concept brings together roastery and eatery—a place to discover coffee, meet people, work, and stay a little longer.
